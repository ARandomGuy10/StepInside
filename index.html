Find out now, not after the walls are up.

On paper every plan looks fine. These are the things people only notice once they stand in the finished room, when changing them means breaking something. In the model, changing them is a click.

Does it feel the way you pictured it?

Walk in through the gate, up the stairs and into every room at eye height. A room of 13 by 15 feet is only a number until you have stood in it.

Walk it yourself, or press Tour and it walks every room for you.

Will your furniture fit, with room to walk past it?

Every room and every piece is to size. Drag the bed to the other wall, type in the width of the sofa you already own, and see what space is left.

Sizes in feet or metres. Nothing passes through a wall.

Which rooms get the sun, and when?

Pick a date and drag the time. The sun follows its real path for your town, so you see which rooms get the morning and where the evening shade falls.

Outdoor lights come on by themselves after sunset.

Which floor, which wall colour?

Click a wall or a floor and give it paint, stone, wood or tile. Or restyle the whole home in one click and see the same room three ways before you order anything.

Seven looks. Undo brings yours back.

Does everyone agree on the same design?

Put two designs side by side from the same spot. Then send one link to your family, architect or builder, and they open that exact view in their browser.

Nothing to install, on a laptop or a phone.

Also in every model

  • Plans with sizes. Each floor flat or in 3D, in feet or metres.
  • Areas. Carpet and built-up area per floor, and plot coverage.
  • A PDF. The plans and the area statement, ready to print.
  • Add and remove. New pieces from a catalogue, or empty a room.
  • Saved views. Keep the spots you want to come back to.
  • Undo. For every change, as far back as you like.

What to send

  • The plan. An architect's drawing or your own sketch, as a photo or a scan.
  • Sizes, if you have them. The plot and any rooms you have measured.
  • How many floors. And which drawing is which floor.
  • What you are unsure about. We will make sure you can see it.
