From your drawing to your front door.

  1. Send your plan

    Make an account, add a photo or scan of the plan and tell us what matters to you.

  2. We build it

    For now a person builds each model by hand, so it is not instant. Your account shows where it stands.

  3. Step inside

    Open it, walk it, change it, and share it with the people building it.

What to send

  • The plan. An architect's drawing or your own sketch, as a photo or a scan.
  • Sizes, if you have them. The plot and any rooms you have measured.
  • How many floors. And which drawing is which floor.
  • What you are unsure about. We will make sure you can see it.
